Deterministic Scoring Weights

Candidate compatibility is evaluated across 5 weighted dimensions totaling exactly 100%.

1. Required Technical Skills Match {{ weights.required_skills }}%
Mandatory competencies identified in the job specification.
2. Preferred / Bonus Skills Match {{ weights.preferred_skills }}%
Nice-to-have tools, domain frameworks, and secondary proficiencies.
3. Work Experience Duration {{ weights.experience }}%
Candidate verified years of experience compared against required minimums.
4. Educational Qualifications {{ weights.education }}%
Hierarchical evaluation of candidate degree level vs required degree level.
5. Textual / Semantic TF-IDF Similarity {{ weights.text_similarity }}%
Cosine similarity between resume text corpus and job posting description.

Ethical AI & Compliance Safeguards

TalentMatch AI is strictly architected to adhere to fair hiring standards:

  • Zero Demographic Attributes: Race, gender, religion, caste, nationality, and age are excluded from candidate matching algorithms.
  • Distinction of Missing Data: Unconfirmed attributes are represented as unavailable rather than penalizing candidates as mismatches.
  • Human Decision-Support: Match scores provide explainable alignment insights, serving as informational indicators rather than automated hiring decisions.
  • Data Confidentiality: Resumes are processed locally in private storage without transmitting personal data to third-party generative APIs.
